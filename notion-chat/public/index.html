Welcome! This interface lets you browse and interact with your Notion workspace.

Quick commands:

  • /databases — list all accessible databases
  • /query <database name> — query a saved database
  • /search <text> — search across your workspace
  • /view <database name> — view database schema
  • /read <page title> — read a page's content
  • /add <database name> | property: value, ... — add entry
  • Or just type naturally to search your workspace
